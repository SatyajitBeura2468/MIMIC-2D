A Beura Labs game

LEVELS

WARDROBE

HOW TO PLAY

  • Move: WASD / arrows / touch stick.
  • E / MIMIC: copy the nearest object. Stay still and you are furniture.
  • Room logic: an object must live in the room you copy it in. A toaster in the bedroom is suspicious. Park next to a real twin for perfect cover.
  • Never move while watched. Never change shape in his view. Cameras see blobs and movers.
  • Pokes: when he walks up to you, freeze. Pass it and he trusts that spot. Pizza box: he is hungry, so it takes longer.
  • Drop the disguise to nibble cookies (exposed!). RUN (Shift) is loud.
  • Eat all the cookies before the timer ends. Hidden power-ups (ghost, speed, freeze, zen, charm) are scattered around the house. Explore!
